Step 5: Validation & Initial Qualification Evaluation
AO II evaluates submitted documentary requirements at the school level.

Applicable Frameworks for Teaching Personnel:
- Natural Vacancy (Teacher I positions): Evaluated under DepEd Quality Standards (DepEd Order No. 7, s. 2023).
- Expanded Career Progression (ECP): Evaluated under DepEd Order No. 19, s. 2025 and DepEd Order No. 24, s. 2025.

AO II Decision (Teaching Personnel):
- Declare Disqualified (DQ): If applicant fails to meet prescribed criteria under DO 7, s. 2023 or DO 19/24, s. 2025 during school-level evaluation.
- Return for Correction: If documentary deficiency is noted.
- Validate Submission (Declare Qualified): If criteria are met, status becomes "Validated by AO II" and forwarded to HRMO for final ranking/approval.

Note on Non-Teaching Personnel:
For non-teaching personnel, the AO II performs initial document completeness check. The Human Resource Management Office (HRMO) is exclusively responsible for evaluating submitted documents and determining whether a non-teaching applicant is Qualified or Disqualified at the division level.

System Result
If Disqualified (DQ):
Status: Disqualified by AO II (Teaching) / HRMO (Non-Teaching)
Disqualification notice and criteria failure remarks sent to personnel.
If Returned:
Status: Returned by AO II
Remarks are sent to personnel for resubmission.
If Validated (Qualified):
Status: Validated by AO II
Transaction forwarded to HRMO for final ranking and approval.
