# Digital 201 — Final Defense Motion Video Prompt (2:30)

## Master prompt (paste this first)

Create a 2-minute-30-second motion-graphics explainer video for **Digital 201**, a web and Android HR information system built for the **Schools Division of Koronadal City (DepEd)**. The video is a step-by-step walkthrough of the system's whole workflow, made for a capstone final defense in front of a panel.

**Style:** clean, modern flat motion graphics in 16:9 at 1920×1080 and 30 fps. Use smooth easing, card and UI-panel animations, icons that draw themselves in, connecting arrows that trace the flow between roles, and light parallax. Keep it professional and calm, not flashy.
**Palette:** deep green `#17472e` (primary), white, soft gray `#f4f6f5`, and a gold accent `#d4a017` for highlights. Use rounded cards and soft shadows.
**Typography:** a bold geometric sans for titles and a clean sans for labels. On-screen text stays short (3–7 words).
**Recurring motif:** a single document card ("201 file") that travels through the workflow from person to person, changing its status badge as it moves.
**Audio:** a warm, clear, professional narrator (neutral English) over light, upbeat corporate background music at a low volume. Add a soft whoosh on transitions and a gentle "tick" when a status badge changes.
**Pronunciation:** say "Digital 201" as **"Digital two-oh-one"**, never "two thousand one". Say "AO II" as **"A-O two"** and "HRMO" as **"H-R-M-O"**.
**Terminology (use exactly):** say "Open items", not vacancies. Say "Comparative Assessment Result", not ranking.

---

## Scene-by-scene

### Scene 1: Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** a cluttered desk with stacks of paper folders. The folders swirl up and compress into one glowing green card labelled **Digital 201**. The logo locks in.
**On-screen:** "From paper folders → one digital 201 file"
**Narration:** "Every teacher and employee has a 201 file. Until now, it lived in paper folders. Meet Digital two-oh-one."

### Scene 2: What it is (0:10–0:22)
**Visual:** a laptop and an Android phone slide in side by side, both showing the same dashboard. Small icons orbit them: records, documents, applications, notifications.
**On-screen:** "Web + Android · One account"
**Narration:** "Digital two-oh-one is a web and mobile HR system for the Schools Division of Koronadal City. Personnel manage their records, apply for promotions, and track every step, on one account."

### Scene 3: The four roles (0:22–0:40)
**Visual:** four character cards pop in one at a time on a horizontal line, each with an icon and a one-line duty. Arrows then connect them left to right.
1. **Personnel**: "Records, uploads, applications"
2. **AO II**: "School accounts & document checks"
3. **HRMO**: "Promotion review & final approval"
4. **System Administrator**: "Accounts, access, system health"
**Narration:** "There are four roles. Personnel keep their records and apply. The AO two manages their school and checks documents. The HRMO runs the promotion review and gives final approval. And the System Administrator manages accounts, access, and system health, without taking part in approvals."

### Scene 4: Getting in (0:40–0:55)
**Visual:** an AO II card sends an "account request" envelope to HR. An HRMO card can also bulk-import a spreadsheet, shown as rows sliding in. Next is a sign-in screen, then a phone receiving a **6-digit code**. A Privacy Notice panel appears with a checkbox ticking.
**On-screen:** "Account request → Sign in → Device code → Privacy consent"
**Narration:** "Accounts are requested by the AO two or imported by HR. On a new device, the system emails a six-digit code. And on first sign-in, every user reads and accepts the Data Privacy Notice."

### Scene 5: Profile & 201 files (0:55–1:10)
**Visual:** a profile card fills in. A PDS (CS Form 212) PDF drops in, and its fields fly out into the profile: name, sex, civil status, email. The document list shows the status badges **Uploaded → Checked → Approved**.
**On-screen:** "Upload once. Reuse everywhere."
**Narration:** "Personnel upload their documents once, into their digital 201 file. The system can even read the Personal Data Sheet and fill in the details. Each file shows its status: uploaded, checked, or approved."

### Scene 6: Open items & applying (1:10–1:25)
**Visual:** an **Open items** board shows position cards with deadlines and an "Eligible" tick. One card is tapped and a checklist slides open. Documents snap from the 201 file into each slot, then the user presses **Submit application**.
**On-screen:** "Open items → Checklist → Submit"
**Narration:** "When a position opens, it appears under Open items, with its deadline and whether you qualify. Apply, attach each required document straight from your 201 file, and submit."

### Scene 7: Routing & AO II review (1:25–1:40)
**Visual:** the document card travels along a forked path. Teaching personnel go to the **AO II**. Non-teaching personnel, and schools without an AO II, go straight to the **HRMO**. The AO II zooms into a document, marks items ✔ **Verified** or ✖ **Deficient**, and types a remark.
**On-screen:** "Right reviewer, automatically"
**Narration:** "The system routes each application to the right reviewer. For teaching personnel, the AO two checks every requirement, marking each one verified, or deficient with a clear remark."

### Scene 8: Correction loop (1:40–1:52)
**Visual:** the card bounces back to Personnel with a red badge, **Returned for correction**. Only one slot is replaced, and the badge flips to amber, **Waiting for review**. The loop arrow glows.
**On-screen:** "Fix only what's returned"
**Narration:** "If something is missing, the applicant is notified, replaces only that item, and resubmits. No starting over."

### Scene 9: HRMO, CAR & appointment (1:52–2:14)
**Visual:** a promotion-cycle timeline with four steps: **Position → Details → Schedule → Review**. Applicant rows fill with board scores, and a document titled **Comparative Assessment Result** prints out. A slot counter shows only that many candidates being selected. A second, different HRMO stamps **Final Approval**. The position and service record update with a gold glow.
**On-screen:** "Scores → Comparative Assessment Result → Selection → Final approval"
**Narration:** "Verified applications reach the HRMO inside a promotion cycle. Board scores are recorded, and the system generates the Comparative Assessment Result. Candidates are selected within the authorized slots, and a different HRMO gives the final approval, which updates the position and service record."

### Scene 10: Accountability (2:14–2:22)
**Visual:** a scrolling audit-trail timeline with entries ticking in. Notification bells light up on both the phone and the laptop.
**On-screen:** "Every action notified. Every action recorded."
**Narration:** "Every step sends a notification, and every action is recorded in the audit trail."

### Scene 11: Close (2:22–2:30)
**Visual:** all the elements collapse back into the green Digital 201 card, and the logo and tagline appear. Show "Schools Division of Koronadal City" in small text below.
**On-screen:** "Digital 201 — Organized records. Transparent promotions."
**Narration:** "Digital two-oh-one. Organized records, transparent promotions."

**Narration length:** about 360 words, which fits 2:30 at a calm 145 words per minute.
