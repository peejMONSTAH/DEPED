# Personnel classification

AO II (`AO_II`) and HR staff (`HRMO`) are **non-teaching personnel** for workforce classification and reporting, alongside `NON_TEACHING_PERSONNEL`. Do not exclude them from non-teaching dashboard counts merely because their access roles are administrative.

Keep workforce classification separate from authorization: preserve their access roles, permissions, review routing, and assigned-school scope. Do not reclassify their accounts to `NON_TEACHING_PERSONNEL` to fix reporting. Count only personnel records visible within the requesting user's existing scope.
